Green Spine · Data we used

Where each dataset comes from, how we collected it, how the model uses it and which KPIs it feeds.

DataSourceHow we collected itHow we used itKPIs it feeds
Buildings with heights3D BAG (lod12), CC BY 4.0Downloaded through the 3D BAG web service by scripts/fetch_data.py: 3,248 building parts within 400 m of the corridor. Height = roof (70th percentile) minus ground level. The four tiles in data/raw/3dbag/ are stored but not read by the appNo trees inside buildings or within 5 m of a facade. Cast shadows for sun hours. Buildings over 30 m push wind down to the street, which affects the comfort and wellbeing of people walking past: they get canopies and define the facades for the wind indexSun hours, heat stress, wind index, covered ground; indirectly all planting KPIs (fewer plantable cells)
Existing treesRIVM Bomenkaart 2022, 10 m cells, % cover by trees over 2.5 mNational raster clipped by a teammate to the Fellenoord scope plus 250 m (scripts/clip_bomenkaart.py)Each cell becomes a disc with the same area as its tree cover. Counts as today's trees, casts shadows, sets the distance-to-trees (gap) score. Cells with 30% tree cover or more are protected, plus a 3 m root zoneCanopy cover (baseline 9–10%), shaded route, longest gap, existing trees kept, heat stress, wind index, synergy index
RoadsOpenStreetMap, ODbLDownloaded by scripts/fetch_data.py; centre lines widened by road type (3.2 m per tagged lane)No trees on the carriageway or within 2 m of it. Carries the road pergolaPlantable area; covered ground
Green areasOpenStreetMap (grass, parks, gardens, scrub, woodland)Downloaded by scripts/fetch_data.pyOpen ground scores soil 1.0; everything else counts as paving (0.35) and needs engineered soil for large treesTrees in natural soil, engineered soil volume, synergy index (habitat, resilience)
The Dommel and wet groundOpenStreetMap river lineDownloaded by scripts/fetch_data.py; ground within 30 m of the river marked wetEnds the corridor at the river. Wet cells get species that tolerate wet soilSpecies choice; synergy index (connection to the Dommel)
Corridor and walking lineDerived from OpenStreetMapDraft: mid-line of the Fellenoord and Dorgelolaan carriageways down to the Dommel, 909 m long, 30 m either side (5.45 ha). The architect still has to confirm itCorridor area is the denominator for canopy cover. The walking line is where route shade and gaps are measuredCanopy cover, shaded route, longest gap, heat stress
Soil boreholesBRO (Basisregistratie Ondergrond), downloaded from DINOloketCollected by a teammate: 6 boreholes with layer depth, texture and made ground (data/raw/soil/)The team's soil algorithm compares each plant's texture and root-depth needs with every borehole profile. In the app, each tree's species must fit the soil at its nearest usable borehole (score 0.6 or more). One borehole is unusable (its record starts at 0.8 m)Species choice and the soil-fit column in the schedule. Barely changes results: only European white elm is ruled out
Plant databaseTeam, merged from GBIF, POWO, PlantNet, PFAF and ForwardPlantBuilt by the team: 1,477 plants of all types, 75 of them trees (data/raw/plants/)Input to the soil algorithm: soil texture and root depth per plantSpecies choice
Soil-compatible speciesTeam's soil algorithm (backend/soil.py) on the plant database and the DINOloket boreholesThe algorithm keeps every plant that fits at least one borehole: 680 of 1,477 (trees, shrubs, herbs and grasses), 73 of them trees (data/processed/fellenoord_compatible_plants.csv)The app plants trees only. It takes its species from a list of 20 native trees (data/processed/plants.csv, set up when the app was first built), and the algorithm checks each tree against its nearest borehole: 19 of the 20 pass. Size class sets crown and spacing; genus and family drive the 10-20-30 ruleLargest species share, synergy index (diversity, native share)
Sun positionCalculated (standard solar formulas)21 June at Fellenoord (51.44° N, 5.48° E), every 30 minutes with the sun above 10°Shadows of buildings and crowns, cell by cell and along the routeSun hours, heat score for placement, heat stress
